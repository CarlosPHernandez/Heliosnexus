Our Mission

At Helios Nexus, we're transforming how homeowners go solar by eliminating salespeople and using AI and machine learning to deliver accurate, cost-effective proposals. We're also building an independent EV charging network and have a long-term vision to make space-based solar power a reality, eventually beaming energy to Earth, the moon, and Mars. We're pioneering the future of energy for a sustainable planet and beyond.

Why Invest in Us?

  • Innovative Technology: Our approach is simple, with the use of AI we can predict accurate energy usage, consumption, and future needs.
  • Market Opportunity: We have seen unprecedented expansion potential, with solar capacity doubling in recent years and global PV shipments reaching 564GW in 2023; a 100% increase from 2022 to 2023. This is an unparalleled growth rate in the energy sector. The U.S. residential solar market was valued at 7.45 billion USD in 2023, and is expected to grow to 17.68 billion by 2030, a 14.4% increase.
  • Proven Leadership: Our small but determined team has successfully secured partnerships with local installers and distributors, and we've also secured a $7,500 investment from a local accelerator. With letters of agreement and intent, Helios now has its first couple of sales pending. By cutting costs and maintaining a lean operations model, Helios is on track to become profitable within its first year.
  • Strategic Partnerships: Helios has forged key partnerships with local installers, manufacturers, and distributors, enabling us to significantly reduce the cost of solar solutions. In addition to these business partnerships, we collaborate with educational institutions to facilitate talent placement for students and alumni. Our educational partners include Alamance Community College and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
  • Sustainable Growth: Our business model is straightforward: we leverage economies of scale. With every sale Helios makes, we expand our network of solar-powered homes, allowing us to steadily develop our charging infrastructure. Through a combination of in-house financing, subscriptions, and add-ons, our model offers tailored solutions for everyone.
